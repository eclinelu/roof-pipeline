### 2026-07-26: The 2026-07-23 facet state is SUPERSEDED, not corrected

**Decision:** The 2026-07-23 26-facet state and every diagnostic derived from
it (`pairwise-2026-07-23.json`, `residual-map-2026-07-23.json`,
`oversegmentation-2026-07-23.json`, `coverage-comparison-2026-07-23.json`) stay
on disk unchanged, marked SUPERSEDED. They are not corrected, not regenerated,
and not deleted. The canonical replacement is `canonical-2026-07-26.json` plus
its `.npz`, built under `probability=1.0` with geometry persisted per R1.

**Why (Emmett's words, 2026-07-26):** *"Do NOT delete or overwrite the
2026-07-23 files. They stay on disk marked SUPERSEDED with the reason. They are
the evidence that the old state was one sample from a distribution, and that
record is worth more than a corrected file."*

That state is permanently unrecoverable for two compounding reasons: the fit
that produced it was nondeterministic (see
2026-07-26-ransac-nondeterminism-probability-1.md), and its geometry was never
written to disk, only summary rows of pitch, gross, occluded and net, which is
not enough to reconstruct a plane. Neither re-running nor reloading can bring
it back.

A corrected file would imply the old numbers were a mistake in arithmetic. They
were not. They were a correct computation over one draw from a distribution the
instrument was silently sampling. Keeping the file preserves that distinction,
which is the whole finding.

**What the replacement is NOT:** the deterministic pipeline does not reproduce
the old state, it produces a different decomposition. Blob 0 now resolves into
two clean facets (62,496 points at 23.59 deg, quality 0.758; 14,099 points at
10.51 deg, quality 1.597) where the old run produced one bloated fit plus a
109,972-point spanning plane rejected at quality 3.927. The old state had 26
facets, the new one has 25. Facet INDICES do not correspond between the two and
must never be cross-referenced.

**Measured, not assumed:** the new partition owns 22,887 FEWER points in blob 0
than the old one, which raised a fair question about whether better meaning had
been bought with worse coverage. It had not. Over the identical query box, with
an identical building denominator (delta exactly 0.0000 cu^2, the validity
check), explained area rose from 68.77 to 72.81 percent and residual fell from
4.252 to 3.576 cu^2. Whole-roof coverage rose from 93.14 to 93.51 percent
against the same 202.171 cu^2 denominator.

**Not affected:** the eight main facets, the frozen 313.188 cu^2 total, and all
pre-registration evidence. At `probability=1.0` the main facets match
`preregistered-2026-07-18.json` to 0.00043 deg worst case, the same worst-case
delta the old default produced. Main-facet discovery was already bit-identical,
because a 200k-point subsample with dominant planes is over-determined enough
that the early stop never faced a close call. `preregistered-2026-07-18.json`
was read-only throughout and was never edited.

**Rejected:** Regenerating the 2026-07-23 files under the new pipeline and
keeping the old filenames. That would silently replace one state's numbers with
another's under a name that implies continuity, and would destroy the only
surviving evidence of the defect.

**Evidence:** `canonical-2026-07-26.json`, `blob0-coverage-2026-07-26.json`,
`coverage-map-2026-07-26.json`, `threading-2026-07-26.json`, and the retained
2026-07-23 files.

**Cost if wrong:** Disk, plus the standing risk that someone reads a
2026-07-23 number as current. Mitigated by the SUPERSEDED marker carried inside
`canonical-2026-07-26.json` itself, so the replacement names what it replaces.
